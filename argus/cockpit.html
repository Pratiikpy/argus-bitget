17/20 probes met their bar. 3 did not and are named rather than averaged away: t2-sharpe-mdd-winrate (UNDEFINED), t2-open-evaluation (WEAK), t2-risk-control (WEAK). The plan's vocabulary calls a probe that runs IMPLEMENTED, never OWNED — OWNED needs a named baseline reproduced and beaten, and no probe here claims one. The Track 2 shortfalls are one shortfall: the desk has never proposed a position, so nothing settles (no Sharpe, no drawdown), nothing is offered to the risk layer (its gates are proven over the swept domain but unexercised live), and nothing reaches the venue (no takeover rate). The confidence floor is not the cause and has never run — the desk returns on quantity<=0 before reaching it, so every risk record names the no-exposure gate (461 record(s) on the current log). The desk HAS been offered tradeable sessions: 265 of 543 decisions were taken in one, and 260 of those carried a directional lean rather than declining to call the direction — yet none opened exposure. That is a statement about the desk, not about the sampling, and it is the pre-registered hypothesis in data/protocol_commitments.jsonl being tested: the commitment says that if the desk abstains through a full RTH week, the deliberation-cost hurdle is too high to trade this universe at all, and that is the finding to publish rather than a lowered bar.
